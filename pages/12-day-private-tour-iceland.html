Seasonal Guide

Best Time for a 12 Day Iceland Tour

A private 12 day grand tour can be enjoyed throughout the year, with each season bringing its own character to the journey.

Summer — June to August

Long daylight hours, easier access around the Ring Road, green landscapes and generous time for scenic stops, photography and spontaneous detours.

Winter — November to February

Possible with flexible planning. Northern Lights potential, snow-covered landscapes and dramatic winter light create a unique journey, though the route may need adjustments for weather and road conditions.

Spring / Autumn — March to May / September to October

Fewer travellers, beautiful changing light, dramatic skies and ideal conditions for landscape photography. A favourite season for experienced travellers seeking a more peaceful Iceland.
