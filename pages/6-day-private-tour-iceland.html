South Iceland Private Journey

A Private 6 Day Journey Through South Iceland

This tour is designed for travelers who want to explore the full breadth of South Iceland without rushing. Over six days, you will discover the iconic Golden Circle, the dramatic South Coast with its waterfalls and black sand beaches, the glacier area around Skaftafell, and the breathtaking Jokulsarlon Glacier Lagoon with Diamond Beach.

Unlike shorter itineraries, this private journey gives you time to venture further east toward Hofn and Vestrahorn, travel at a relaxed pace, and enjoy the flexibility to adapt the route as you go. Every stop, every detour and every moment is guided by your interests, with a dedicated private driver-guide who handles the planning and driving so you can focus on the experience.

Detailed Itinerary

Day by Day in South Iceland

Each day is designed to give you a rich, unhurried experience of Iceland's south. Your driver-guide will adapt the timing and stops to match your interests, the weather and the season.

Day 1

Arrival, Reykjavik or Golden Circle

Private pickup from Reykjavik or Keflavik Airport. Begin your journey with the Golden Circle, including Thingvellir National Park, the Geysir geothermal area and Gullfoss waterfall, depending on arrival time and your preferences.

Thingvellir National Park Geysir Gullfoss Private pickup Flexible start
Day 2

South Coast Waterfalls and Vik

Travel along the South Coast with stops at Seljalandsfoss, Skogafoss and scenic coastal viewpoints before reaching the Vik area. Your driver-guide will recommend the best photo spots and hidden angles along the way.

Seljalandsfoss Skogafoss Eyjafjallajokull views Vik Coastal landscapes
Day 3

Black Sand Beaches and Glacier Landscapes

Explore Reynisfjara Black Sand Beach with its dramatic basalt columns and roaring Atlantic waves. Continue east through lava fields and glacial outwash plains toward the Skaftafell or glacier region.

Reynisfjara Dyrholaey Lava fields Glacier views Skaftafell area
Day 4

Glacier Lagoon, Diamond Beach and Southeast Iceland

Visit Jokulsarlon Glacier Lagoon and the nearby Diamond Beach, where icebergs glisten on black volcanic sand. Enjoy the floating ice sculptures and dramatic southeast landscapes. Continue toward Hofn or Vestrahorn if the route and season allow.

Jokulsarlon Glacier Lagoon Diamond Beach Vatnajokull views Hofn Vestrahorn
Day 5

Scenic Return Through the South Coast

Return west at a relaxed pace with flexible stops, hidden viewpoints and optional experiences depending on weather, season and your personal interests. Your guide knows the quiet spots that larger tours miss.

Hidden stops Flexible photography Waterfalls Canyons Volcanic landscapes
Day 6

Final Icelandic Landscapes and Departure

Enjoy a final scenic route back to Reykjavik or Keflavik Airport. The day can be adapted to flight times, city sightseeing, the Blue Lagoon area, Reykjanes Peninsula or extra South Coast stops you may have missed.

Flexible ending Reykjavik Keflavik Airport Reykjanes Peninsula Optional Blue Lagoon area

When to Go

Best Time to Travel

The 6 day South Iceland tour is available year-round. Each season offers a completely different character and set of experiences.

  • Summer (Jun – Aug)

    Long daylight hours, easier road access, greener landscapes and the chance to explore the highlands and glacier areas in ideal conditions.

  • Winter (Oct – Mar)

    Dramatic snow-covered landscapes, possible Northern Lights, ice cave access (seasonal), frozen waterfalls and a quieter, more intimate atmosphere.

  • Shoulder Seasons (Apr – May / Sep)

    Fewer travelers, strong photography conditions, changing colors and a good balance of daylight and atmospheric weather patterns.
