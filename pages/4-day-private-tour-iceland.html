The 4-Day Journey Path

Follow the progression of your private expedition across Iceland's most iconic landscapes. Every day is optimized for maximum discovery.

  1. Day 01

    Arrival & Reykjavik

    Transfer from Keflavik Airport via the soothing geothermal waters of the Blue Lagoon. Settle into Reykjavik and explore its vibrant culinary and cultural scene.

    Drive:
    50 KM
    Pace:
    Relaxed
    Highlight:
    Geothermal Spa
  2. Day 02

    The Golden Circle

    Walk between continental plates at Thingvellir National Park, watch the powerful Strokkur geyser erupt, and marvel at the massive Gullfoss waterfall.

    Drive:
    250 KM
    Trail Time:
    2.5 Hrs
    Highlight:
    Tectonic Rift
  3. Day 03

    Glacier Lagoon

    Venture deep into the South Coast to witness giant icebergs floating in Jökulsárlón lagoon and washing up on the volcanic black sands of Diamond Beach.

    Drive:
    380 KM
    Trail Time:
    3.0 Hrs
    Highlight:
    Ice Diamonds
  4. Day 04

    Waterfalls & Extraction

    Conclude your journey by visiting the majestic Skógafoss and Seljalandsfoss waterfalls before your private driver safely escorts you back to the airport.

    Drive:
    220 KM
    Pace:
    Moderate
    Highlight:
    Cascading Falls
