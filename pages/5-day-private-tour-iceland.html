Route Breakdown & Analytics

Scroll down to navigate through the itinerary chronologically.

1

Arrival & Thermal Vectors

Upon landing at Keflavik International Airport, you will interface with your private driver-guide. The journey commences with an immersive transfer to the Blue Lagoon, analyzing the geothermal sub-surfaces while recovering from your flight. Later, we secure logistics in Reykjavik's coastal hub.

Driving Distance50 km
Est. Driving Time45 mins
Primary TargetReykjanes Peninsula
Geological FeatureSilica-rich Geothermal Water
2

Borgarfjörður Volcanic Corridor

We transit north via the Borgarfjörður basin. Key research assets include Deildatunguhver, the highest-flowing hot spring in Europe, and the Hraunfossar cascades, where water emerges directly from a subterranean basaltic lava shelf. Overnight stay in the Skagafjörður valley.

Driving Distance310 km
Est. Driving Time4.5 hours
Primary TargetSkagafjörður Basin
Geological FeatureLava-field Filtration System
3

Fjord Mechanics & Maritime Hubs

An advanced exploration of the Tröllaskagi Peninsula. We trace the structural basalt cuts of the northern fjords, stopping at Siglufjörður's historical deep-water port. The day concludes in Akureyri, Iceland's northern capital, observing microclimates and sub-arctic botany.

Driving Distance160 km
Est. Driving Time2.5 hours
Primary TargetEyjafjörður Fjord
Geological FeatureGlacial Fjord Excavation
4

Basalt Monoliths & Sea Erosion

Field analysis of the Vatnsnes peninsula. We encounter Hvítserkur, a 15-meter volcanic plug eroded by marine wave dynamics, resembling a dragon drinking from the arctic surf. Geothermal recovery session at the Grettislaug hot spring before mapping our vector back to Reykjavik.

Driving Distance280 km
Est. Driving Time3.8 hours
Primary TargetVatnsnes Monolith
Geological FeatureMarine Volcanic Dike Plug
5

Final Vector & Extraction

Final itinerary debriefing in the capital. Your private driver-guide executes a strategic transfer back to Keflavík International Airport. Optional atmospheric capture sessions available depending on flight telemetry.

Driving Distance50 km
Est. Driving Time45 mins
Primary TargetKeflavik Terminal
Geological FeatureReykjanes Basalt Plains
