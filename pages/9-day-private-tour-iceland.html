The Journey

The Journey in 5 Cinematic Scenes

Each chapter of this private tour unfolds like a scene from a film, shaped by Iceland's changing light and landscapes.

01
First Light in Iceland

Arrival and the Golden Circle

Your private driver-guide meets you at Keflavik Airport or Reykjavík. Depending on your arrival, the journey begins with a relaxed introduction to Iceland — through the moss-covered lava fields of Reykjanes, the colourful rooftops of Reykjavík, or straight into the Golden Circle's tectonic landscapes.

  • Private pickup
  • Reykjavík or Reykjanes
  • Flexible start
  • First Icelandic landscapes
02
Waterfalls and Volcanic Coast

The Dramatic South Coast

Follow the coast east where waterfalls plunge from ancient sea cliffs and black sand beaches meet the North Atlantic. Walk behind Seljalandsfoss, feel the spray of Skógafoss and explore Reynisfjara Black Sand Beach with its basalt columns and roaring waves.

  • Seljalandsfoss
  • Skógafoss
  • Reynisfjara Black Sand Beach
  • Vík
03
Ice, Black Sand and Glacier Country

Glacier Lagoon and Diamond Beach

Continue east to the realm of ice. Jökulsárlón Glacier Lagoon drifts with ancient icebergs, while Diamond Beach scatters frozen crystals across black volcanic sand. The vastness of Vatnajökull, Europe's largest glacier, forms the backdrop to one of Iceland's most unforgettable landscapes.

  • Jökulsárlón Glacier Lagoon
  • Diamond Beach
  • Vatnajökull glacier views
  • Photography paradise
04
The Long Scenic Return

Hidden Stops and Flexible Discovery

The return journey west follows the same coast but feels completely different. With extra time built into the itinerary, your driver-guide will find hidden canyons, lesser-known waterfalls, photography viewpoints and stops that match the day's weather and light.

  • Hidden viewpoints
  • Weather-based routing
  • Canyons and waterfalls
  • Flexible private planning
05
Snæfellsnes and the Western Edge

Iceland in Miniature

The Snæfellsnes Peninsula delivers a stunning finale. Here, volcanic craters, lava fields, fishing villages and the iconic Kirkjufell mountain sit beneath the glacier-capped Snæfellsjökull volcano. It is a landscape that captures the full spirit of Iceland in a single day.

  • Kirkjufell
  • Arnarstapi
  • Búðakirkja black church
  • Snæfellsjökull glacier views

Seasonal Guide

Best Time for a 9 Day Iceland Tour

A private 9 day Iceland tour can be enjoyed year-round, with each season offering a unique atmosphere and experience.

Summer — June to August

Long daylight hours, easier road conditions, greener landscapes and more time for scenic stops. Perfect for photographers and families who want maximum flexibility.

Winter — November to February

Snow-covered landscapes, frozen waterfalls, dramatic atmosphere and the possibility of Northern Lights. Ice cave add-ons are available depending on conditions.

Spring / Autumn — March to May / September to October

Fewer crowds, beautiful low-angle light, changing colours and strong conditions for landscape photography. A favourite season for experienced travellers.
