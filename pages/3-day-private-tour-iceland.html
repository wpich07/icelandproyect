Leave The Office Behind

Trade your desk for dirt trails and glacial ice. This intensive 3-day private tour is built for those who crave authentic wilderness. Led by an expert driver-guide, you will navigate raw volcanic landscapes and experience the unfiltered power of Iceland's nature. Scroll down to initiate the route.

Stage 01

Tectonic Fissures

We leave the city limits and head straight into the rift valley of Thingvellir. Hike between the North American and Eurasian tectonic plates before confronting the erupting Strokkur geyser and the glacial floods of Gullfoss.

Terrain TypeVolcanic Fissure

Stage 02

Basalt & Ash

Descend to the rugged South Coast. Prepare to get soaked behind the roaring curtain of Seljalandsfoss. Later, navigate the harsh, wind-battered shores of Reynisfjara, where massive basalt columns meet the Atlantic surf.
