A Deeper Private Journey Through Iceland

This tour is designed for travelers who want more margin, more landscapes and more flexibility. Over eight days, you explore the Golden Circle, the full South Coast, the Glacier Lagoon and Diamond Beach, the Snæfellsnes Peninsula, and the Reykjanes Peninsula — all with the freedom to linger where the landscape moves you most.

Eight days give you what shorter tours cannot: the ability to slow down. Your private driver-guide adapts the route to the weather, the light and your interests. Extra time at waterfalls. Route Chapters

The 8 Day Route Experience

Four visual chapters that trace your private expedition across Iceland's most iconic landscapes, from tectonic divides to glacier-carved shores.

Chapter 1

Golden Circle & First Landscapes

Your journey begins with Iceland's most celebrated route — Þingvellir National Park, the Geysir geothermal area and Gullfoss waterfall. Depending on arrival time, you can also explore the Reykjanes Peninsula or the colorful streets of Reykjavík before settling into your first evening in Iceland.

Þingvellir National Park Geysir Gullfoss Reykjanes Peninsula Reykjavík
Chapter 2

South Coast Waterfalls & Black Sand Beaches

Travel the dramatic South Coast with stops at Seljalandsfoss, where you can walk behind the cascading water, and Skógafoss with its powerful curtain of foam. Continue to Reynisfjara Black Sand Beach, where basalt columns and roaring Atlantic waves create one of Iceland's most iconic landscapes.

Seljalandsfoss Skógafoss Reynisfjara Dyrhólaey Vík
Chapter 3

Glacier Lagoon & Southeast Iceland

Venture east to Jökulsárlón Glacier Lagoon, where icebergs calve from Breiðamerkurjökull and drift slowly toward the sea. Walk on Diamond Beach as crystal-clear ice glistens against black volcanic sand. Explore the vast Vatnajökull region and the landscapes around Höfn, with time for photography and optional glacier activities.

Jökulsárlón Glacier Lagoon Diamond Beach Vatnajökull Höfn area Skaftafell
Chapter 4

Snæfellsnes Peninsula & Flexible Return

Explore the Snæfellsnes Peninsula — Iceland in miniature — with Kirkjufell, Arnarstapi's basalt cliffs, the black church at Búðakirkja and views of Snæfellsjökull glacier. Your final day is fully flexible, with options for Reykjavík, the Blue Lagoon area, Reykjanes Peninsula or coastal photography stops on the way to Keflavik Airport.

Kirkjufell Arnarstapi Búðakirkja Snæfellsjökull Blue Lagoon area

When to Go

Best Time for an 8 Day Iceland Tour

This tour is available year-round. Each season brings a completely different character to Iceland's southern and western landscapes.

Summer (Jun – Aug)

Long daylight hours, easier road access, greener landscapes and more time for extended scenic stops across the South Coast and Snæfellsnes.

Winter (Oct – Mar)

Northern Lights possibilities, dramatic snow-covered landscapes, frozen waterfalls and seasonal ice cave access under Vatnajökull.

Spring / Autumn

Fewer travelers, strong photography conditions, changing light and flexible weather planning across all four regions.
